From finance challenges to actionable solutions

Illustrative examples of approaches to financial control, liquidity management, and digital finance transformation. No confidential information or client-specific outcomes are disclosed.

Liquidity and working capital

Challenge: Limited visibility into future cash flows and competing payment obligations.

Approach: Develop a rolling 13-week cash forecast, prioritize payments by risk and urgency, and review collections weekly.

Suggested measures: Forecast accuracy, coverage of due obligations, and days sales outstanding.

Payment governance and authority

Challenge: Overlapping responsibility for initiating, reviewing, and authorizing payments.

Approach: Define an authority matrix, segregate duties, and require supporting documentation for each payment.

Suggested measures: Authorized-payment rate, exceptions, and payment-cycle time.

Reporting and finance transformation

Challenge: Slow management reporting and inconsistent data across systems.

Approach: Standardize KPI definitions, reconcile data sources, and build management dashboards.

Suggested measures: Reporting turnaround time, reconciliation differences, and close timeliness.

These are illustrative frameworks, not client testimonials or claims of achieved outcomes. Verified case studies should only be published with appropriate approvals.
