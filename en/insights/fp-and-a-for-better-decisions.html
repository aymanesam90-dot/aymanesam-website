FP&A: From Reporting to Better Decisions

Ayman Essam · 2026

Strong FP&A is not limited to producing budgets and monthly reports. Its real value is connecting financial performance with the decisions management needs to make.

A useful finance function explains what changed, why it changed, what is likely to happen next, and which actions can improve the outcome.
